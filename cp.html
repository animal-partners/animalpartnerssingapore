Customised Animal-Assisted Experiences

Looking for something beyond a standard programme? Whether you have specific learning objectives, participant needs, themes, or outcomes in mind, we can work with you to design a customised animal-assisted experience tailored to your group.

From educational workshops and team bonding sessions to wellness, therapeutic, or engagement-based activities, our programmes can be thoughtfully adapted to suit different ages, group sizes, settings, and accessibility needs. Whether you are planning for schools, corporate teams, community groups, seniors, or participants with different needs, we would be happy to explore a programme that best fits your needs.

Our Emphasis on Psychosocial Education

Our animal-assisted psychosocial education programmes are designed to support emotional wellbeing, social-emotional learning, communication, confidence-building, and meaningful interpersonal connections through guided interactions with animals.

Using carefully curated activities and facilitated discussions, participants are encouraged to explore topics such as empathy, emotional regulation, resilience, self-awareness, teamwork, stress management, and positive social behaviours in a safe and engaging environment.

Common Focus Areas:

Educational Focus

Tailored for schools, student enrichment, and youth-at-risk or vulnerable populations to foster resilience and social growth.

Community Focus

Designed for seniors, intergenerational groups, persons with additional needs, and corporate wellbeing initiatives.

Each session is thoughtfully adapted based on participants’ ages, needs, comfort levels, and desired outcomes, with animal welfare and participant wellbeing at the heart of every programme. Get in touch with us to start creating your customised experience today.
