Ensuring Quality and Safety

High-quality animal-assisted therapy (AAT) and activities (AAA) depend on the seamless partnership between the animal and the handler. Our evaluation services provide organizations with an objective, expert assessment of their human-animal teams to ensure they meet professional and ethical standards.

Drawing on our team's background in **psychological science** and **animal welfare**, we look beyond basic obedience. We assess the emotional connection, the handler's ability to "read" their animal, and the suitability of the team for specific target populations (such as seniors, children, or persons with additional needs).

Our Evaluation Framework

We provide comprehensive audit reports and feedback for:

  • Team Readiness: Assessing if a new handler and animal are ready to begin field work.
  • Programme Audits: Evaluating existing animal therapy programmes for safety and effectiveness.
  • Welfare Checks: Ensuring animals are not experiencing burnout or distress during sessions.
  • Skill Development: Identifying specific areas where handlers can improve their facilitation techniques.

Whether you are a healthcare facility, a school, or an independent therapy group, our evaluations provide the peace of mind that your services are safe for the humans involved and respectful of the animals who partner with you.
