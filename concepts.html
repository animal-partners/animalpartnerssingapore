Under Animal Partners Singapore,

We deploy frameworks custom-tailored to specific communities, focuses, and target demographics. Explore our primary core concepts and branding arms below:

CHAC

Our foundational consultancy interface specializing in animal welfare parameters, standard operational definitions, and ecosystem infrastructure.

CAAI

The standard-bearer for therapeutic community outreach, providing specialized, high-safeguard senior visits and baseline animal companion touchpoints.

Project Integrate

An interactive, psychosocial framework empowering youth-at-risk and justice-impacted individuals with critical, data-backed life skills.

SilverConnect

Nurturing active aging, cognitive stimulation, and comfort for older adults by uniting animal activities with therapeutic horticulture.

Tactile Tails

A specialized, multi-sensory concept combining experiential arts, hands-on craft workshops, and direct animal interactions.

Resili-Hens

An innovative poultry-assisted activity arm using chicken behavioral mechanics for grounding, didactic learning, and sensory engagement.
