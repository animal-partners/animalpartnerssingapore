Empowering Sustainable Human-Animal Interaction

Beyond direct delivery, we work with organizations to build internal capacity and create safe, welfare-centered environments. Our consultancy services leverage industry standards and evidence-based practices to help you maximize the positive impact of your animal-related initiatives.

Animal Space Setup

Expert guidance on designing and establishing animal-friendly spaces that prioritize safety, hygiene, and species-specific welfare needs.

Animal Team Evaluation

Comprehensive assessment of human-animal teams to ensure competence, ethical interactions, and alignment with therapeutic goals.

Equipping & Training

Specialized workshops and training modules designed to empower your staff and volunteers with the skills needed for successful animal-assisted work.

Ready to amplify your impact?

Whether you are starting a new project or looking to evaluate an existing programme, our team is here to support your journey towards excellence in animal-assisted services.
