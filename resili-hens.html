Chicken Interactions for Community Well-being

Resili-Hens is a ground-up initiative incorporating chicken-assisted interventions. Widely recognized for their rhythmic, grounding behavior and distinct sensory profiles, our animal partners help participants unlock deep mindfulness, biological curiosity, and regulation.

Through specialized tactile work, caregiving workflows, and structured observations, we invite diverse demographics to build patience, soft skills, and community resilience alongside our feathered partners.

Didactic Education Sessions

Highly structured, classroom-style interactive modules where participants discover life cycles, avian biology, and behavioral traits - translating technical concepts into relatable life metaphors for human resilience.

Active AAA

Dynamic, hands-on Animal-Assisted Activities where groups make things for or alongside our animal partners.

Facility/Institutional Visits

Visits that prompt laughter, boost gentle movement, and stimulate nostalgic conversations. Find out more over at CAAI.
