Play with Purpose

Play! And Learn combines the therapeutic benefits of play with positive interactions with animals to create a safe, supportive, and engaging environment for children and participants to express themselves, build confidence, and develop emotional and social skills.

Through guided activities, sensory experiences, creative play, and gentle interactions with animal companions, participants are encouraged to explore emotions, strengthen communication skills, develop self-regulation, and build meaningful connections in a natural and non-judgemental setting.

Who It's For

We tailor our Play! And Learn programmes for various enrichment and therapeutic settings including:

  • Children and youths
  • Early intervention and enrichment settings
  • Participants with additional support needs
  • Families and caregiver-child bonding
  • Schools, community groups, and therapeutic settings

Simply share with us your objectives, participant profile, preferred animal companion(s), budget, and session requirements, and our team will work with you to curate a meaningful and engaging programme that fits your needs perfectly.
