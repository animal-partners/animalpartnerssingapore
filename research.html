A Culture of Inquiry

At Animal Partners Singapore, research is not just a secondary focus—it is the foundation of every programme we deliver. By integrating rigorous scientific inquiry with practical, community-based implementation, we ensure our interventions are safe, effective, and ethically sound.

Our team members are actively engaged in the academic community, pursuing advanced studies and contributing to peer-reviewed literature. We believe that by documenting the outcomes of our work, we can elevate the standards of animal-assisted services across the region.

Partnership Opportunities

We invite universities, independent scholars, and industry partners to explore collaborative possibilities with us:

Impact Evaluation

Collaborating on quantitative and qualitative studies to measure the efficacy of our animal-assisted and eco-wellness programmes.

Industrial Standards

Working with organizations to develop and test circular economy frameworks, such as our Readiness Index aligned with ISO standards.

Whether you are looking for a site to conduct data collection, a partner for a grant application, or technical expertise in animal care and psychological science, we are ready to collaborate. Reach out to our team to start a conversation about your next research project.
