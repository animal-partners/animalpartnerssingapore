Advancing Knowledge Together

At Animal Partners Singapore, we are committed to an evidence-based approach. We are always welcoming of collaboration and research opportunities that align with our mission of enhancing human-animal relationships and promoting environmental sustainability.

We actively partner with academic institutions, independent researchers, and industry experts to validate the impact of our programmes and contribute to the broader scientific community. Whether it is conducting longitudinal studies on eco-wellness or developing new industrial standards for upcycling, we believe that collaborative data leads to better practice.

How We Collaborate

We offer various avenues for support and partnership:

  • Data Collection: Access to our diverse programme participants and settings for approved research projects.
  • Technical Consultancy: Expert input on research design involving animal welfare or circular economy frameworks.
  • Joint Grant Proposals: Partnering on initiatives that require a practical, field-based implementation partner.
  • Student Internships: Providing opportunities for students in Psychology, Sustainability, or Animal Science to gain hands-on research experience.

If you have a research proposal or an idea for a collaborative project, we would love to hear from you. Let’s work together to create measurable, positive change.
