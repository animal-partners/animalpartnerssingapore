Advancing Knowledge Together

At Animal Partners Singapore, we are committed to an evidence-based approach. We are always welcoming of collaboration and research opportunities that align with our mission of enhancing human-animal relationships and promoting community well-being.

We actively partner with academic institutions, independent researchers, and industry experts to validate the impact of our programmes and contribute to the broader scientific community. Whether it is conducting longitudinal studies on wellness or developing new industry standards for animal-assisted services, we believe that collaborative data leads to better practice.

How We Collaborate

We offer various avenues for support and partnership:

  • Technical Consultancy: Expert input on research design involving animal-assisted services
  • Joint Grant Proposals: Partnering on initiatives that require a practical, field-based implementation partner.
  • Student Internships: Providing opportunities for students in Psychology or Animal Science to gain hands-on research experience.
  • Synergistic Pilots: Partnering with well-being professionals to explore how diverse therapeutic modalities can synergise with animal-assisted services for enhanced outcomes (i.e. Mindfulness Oriented AAA, Applied Drama x AAA)

If you have a research proposal or an idea for a collaborative project, we would love to hear from you. Let’s work together to create measurable, positive change.
