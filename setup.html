Designing for Discovery & Welfare

Setting up a space for animals involves more than just housing; it requires a deep understanding of species-specific needs, safety standards, and user interaction. Whether you are looking to integrate animals into a school, community center, or commercial space, we provide end-to-end consultancy to ensure your setup is both functional and sustainable.

Our approach bridges the gap between technical animal husbandry and meaningful human engagement. We focus on creating "Discovery Spaces" where people can learn and interact safely while ensuring the animals remain healthy, enriched, and stress-free.

Strategic Consulting

From the initial blueprint to the final operational walkthrough, we guide you through:

Infrastructure & Safety

Drafting layouts that optimize biosecurity, manage odors, and ensure the safety of both visitors and animal residents.

Compliance & Care

Establishing standardized operating procedures (SOPs) for daily care, health monitoring, and regulatory alignment.

Every environment is unique. We work closely with your facility managers and stakeholders to curate a space that reflects your organization's mission while upholding the highest standards of animal welfare.
